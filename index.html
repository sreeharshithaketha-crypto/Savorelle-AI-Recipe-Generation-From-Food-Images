About Savorelle

One workflow from ingredients to shopping list

Add the ingredients you have by hand, then edit quantities so recipes are matched against what is really in your kitchen.

From that inventory it generates recipes with ingredients, preparation steps, instructions, time and difficulty, respects your diet and allergies, and carries each choice through meal planning to a shopping list of missing items.

The pipeline
1Add Enter ingredients with quantities. Review or remove items before confirming them.
2Generate Recipes are ranked by how much of each one your inventory already covers.
3Schedule Assign any recipe to a day and meal in the weekly planner.
4Compare Scheduled recipe quantities are checked against inventory.
5Shop A list of missing items is produced. Mark them bought and inventory updates.

How to use this module

Choose a photo to identify the dish and get ingredient suggestions, or enter an ingredient, quantity and unit below. Review the list, adjust quantities or remove items, then select Confirm to inventory.

When configured, photos are sent to OpenAI for analysis. Savorelle does not store uploads. OpenAI's standard API abuse-monitoring logs may retain submitted content for up to 30 days; API data is not used to train models by default. Use may incur API charges on the server operator's account. Selected allergies and foods to avoid are used to withhold matching ingredient suggestions. Image analysis cannot verify hidden ingredients or guarantee allergy safety.
